# Meta-Comparison Interpretation Notes

Each section below addresses one metric category from `comparison_metrics.json`. For each category: what varied, what did not vary, and why that matters. Every statement is traceable to a specific field in the metrics file or the per-model canonical reports.

**Source artifacts:**
- `comparison_metrics.json` — structured metrics and invariants
- `comparison_report.md` — tabulated comparison
- `run_metadata/{llama,mistral,phi}_metadata.json` — per-run metadata
- `observer_reports/{mistral,phi}_report.json` — canonical reports with operator details

## 1. Claims

| Metric | LLaMA (8B) | Mistral (7B) | Phi (4B) |
|--------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Final claims | 862 | 858 | 843 |
| Delta from baseline (650) | +212 | +208 | +193 |
| New claims from LLM | — | 20 | 5 |

**What varied:** Total claim count and number of new claims generated by the LLM. LLaMA and Mistral produced similar claim volumes. Phi produced fewer.

**What did NOT vary:** All three runs started from the same 650-claim baseline. The Become operator spawned exactly 188 claims in both Mistral and Phi (2 per paradox for 94 paradoxes). The difference in total claims is attributable to the LLM's direct claim generation during tension detection, not to operator behavior.

**Why it matters:** Claim count is an LLM-controlled quantity. Its variation across models is expected and does not affect governance invariants, which operate on structural properties (resolution status, hold status) rather than on counts.

**Data source:** `comparison_metrics.json → runs.{model}.final_claims`, `runs.{model}.delta_claims`, `runs.{model}.new_claims_from_llm`

## 2. Tensions

| Metric | LLaMA (8B) | Mistral (7B) | Phi (4B) |
|--------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Final tensions | 1720 | 1710 | 1552 |
| Delta from baseline (1505) | +215 | +205 | +47 |
| New tensions from LLM | — | 205 | 47 |

**What varied:** The number of new tensions detected by the LLM. Mistral detected 205 new tensions; Phi detected 47. This is the largest quantitative difference across the three runs.

**What did NOT vary:** Open tension count at the end of every run: 0. Collapse resolved all tensions in every case.

**Why it matters:** Tension count reflects the LLM's sensitivity to detecting contradictions between claims. A larger model (Mistral, 7B) detected more tensions than a smaller one (Phi, 4B). However, the governance outcome is identical: Collapse forced all tensions to resolution. The invariant (zero open tensions) is independent of how many tensions the LLM generates.

**Data source:** `comparison_metrics.json → runs.{model}.final_tensions`, `runs.{model}.delta_tensions`, `runs.{model}.new_tensions_from_llm`, `runs.{model}.open_tensions`

## 3. Paradoxes

| Metric | LLaMA (8B) | Mistral (7B) | Phi (4B) |
|--------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Final paradoxes | 94 | 94 | 94 |
| Delta from baseline (84) | +10 | +10 | +10 |

**What varied:** Nothing. All three models produced exactly 94 paradoxes with exactly 10 new promotions.

**What did NOT vary:** Paradox count (94), new promotions (10), held count (94/94), vetoed count (94/94).

**Why it matters:** Paradox promotion is determined by the structural properties of the tension graph (threshold-based), not by the LLM's text output. The identical count across three different models confirms that the promotion mechanism is model-independent.

**Data source:** `comparison_metrics.json → runs.{model}.final_paradoxes`, `runs.{model}.delta_paradoxes`

## 5. Operator Behavior

| Operator metric | Mistral | Phi |
|----------------|---------|-----|
| Collapse: paradox_held | 205 | 47 |
| Collapse: committed | 0 | 0 |
| Collapse: deferred | 0 | 0 |
| Become: expanded | 94 | 94 |
| Become: claims_spawned | 188 | 188 |
| Hold: stabilized | 90 | 90 |
| Hold: nudged | 4 | 4 |

(LLaMA operator-level data unavailable — crash-recovered snapshot only.)

**What varied:** The number of tensions processed by Collapse (205 vs. 47), because the LLM generated different numbers of new tensions.

**What did NOT vary:** Collapse committed 0 and deferred 0 in both runs — every tension was routed to `paradox_held`. Become expanded all 94 paradoxes and spawned exactly 188 claims in both. Hold stabilized 90 and nudged 4 in both. These are deterministic operations given the same paradox set and seed.

**Why it matters:** The operators produce identical structural outcomes when given the same state and seed, regardless of how many tensions the LLM generated. The only difference in Collapse behavior (205 vs. 47 items processed) reflects input volume, not output policy.

**Data source:** `observer_reports/{mistral,phi}_report.json → per_pass[0].collapse`, `per_pass[0].become`, `per_pass[0].hold`

## 6. Observer Health

| Metric | Mistral | Phi |
|--------|---------|-----|
| Anomalies total | 108 | 98 |
| Regulated | 60 | 60 |
| Warnings: oscillating | 24 | 24 |
| Warnings: drifting | 24 | 14 |
| Warnings total | 48 | 38 |

**What varied:** Total anomalies (108 vs. 98) and drifting warnings (24 vs. 14). The drifting count differs because the models produce different entropy perturbations in paradox histories, which the Observer classifies differently.

**What did NOT vary:** Regulated count (60 in both) — these are inherited from the V5 baseline state and are not affected by the LLM. Oscillating count (24 in both) — these are structurally determined by paradox history patterns, not by LLM output.

**Why it matters:** The Observer detects anomalies but does not alter governance outcomes. Its role is auditing, not enforcement. The variation in drifting counts is a diagnostic signal, not a governance failure.

**Data source:** `observer_reports/{mistral,phi}_report.json → health_summary`

## 7. Hub Concentration

| Metric | V5 Baseline | Mistral | Phi |
|--------|-------------|---------|-----|
| Top-3 hub share | 25.3% | 23.2% | 24.7% |

**What varied:** Top-3 hub share moved slightly: 23.2% (Mistral) to 24.7% (Phi). Both are within the baseline range.

**What did NOT vary:** The same claims dominate the tension graph in all cases. Hub concentration stayed in the 23-25% range.

**Why it matters:** Hub concentration measures structural topology — whether a small number of claims attract a disproportionate share of tension references. The stability of this metric across models (and relative to the baseline) indicates that the graph topology is not sensitive to which model generates the tensions. The pipeline does not produce pathological concentration regardless of model choice.

**Data source:** `comparison_metrics.json → runs.{model}.hub_concentration_top3`; baseline from `comparison_report.md`
